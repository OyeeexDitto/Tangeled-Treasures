From Yarn to Memories

It all begins with something as simple as a strand of yarn. Slowly, stitch by stitch, it becomes a flower, a tiny companion, or a little treasure made for someone special. And somewhere along the way, it becomes more than just something handmade — it becomes a reminder of a person, a moment, a feeling, or a memory worth keeping. That's the magic of Tangeled Treasures: turning little pieces of yarn into memories that stay.

🧶

Handmade

Every piece begins with a simple strand of yarn and slowly becomes something that feels alive. Stitch by stitch, every flower, keychain and little character is shaped by hand, with patience, care, and a little bit of imperfection. No two pieces are ever exactly alike — and that's what makes each one yours.

🎁

Made for Gifting

Some gifts are opened, admired, and forgotten. We wanted to make the kind that stays. A tiny bouquet for a birthday, a silly little character for a friend, or something handmade just because you thought of someone — these little treasures are made to carry a feeling long after the wrapping paper is gone.

✨

Custom Creations

Can't find exactly what you're looking for? Let's make it yours. Have a favourite character, a special memory, a pet, a doodle, or simply an idea you've been wishing existed as a crochet piece? Send us a reference photo or tell us what you have in mind. We'll work with you to turn that idea into a one-of-a-kind handmade treasure — made especially for you, or someone you love.
